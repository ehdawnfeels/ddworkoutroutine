What changed and why

  1. Gym is now a full 60 minutes. The old 5:15–6:15 block had to cover the drive there too, which left about 45 minutes. The Grind Log sessions are capped at 60 minutes, so the gym now runs 5:30–6:30 with 15 minutes of travel each side.
  2. Wednesday is a rest day. The old plan skipped the 5:00 wake-up but still had you at the gym at 5:15, which can't work. The Grind Log already lists Wednesday as rest, so you now sleep until 6:00 and take Dustin on a longer walk.
  3. A shower block was missing. You went from the gym straight to walking Dustin and leaving. There's now 20 minutes for a shower, getting dressed and breakfast.
  4. Cooking moved out of the morning. Cooking and packing food in the same 45 minutes as a dog walk was the tightest part of the day. Cook a double batch at dinner and pack tomorrow's lunch from it. Sunday prep covers the rest.
  5. Dinner is a real block and ends by 7:15. The old plan had cooking but no time to eat, so dinner ran into wind-down. Finishing nearly 2 hours before bed is better for sleep.
  6. Dustin gets walked as soon as you're home. He's been alone the longest by 6:00, and the walk also helps you shake off the commute.
  7. The latest lights-out is 9:30, not 10:00, before a 5:00 alarm. See the sleep math below: 10:00 puts you under 7 hours.
  8. Later nights are allowed when the next morning starts later. Wednesday, Friday and Sunday start later, so Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday nights get an extra hour of free time.
  9. Friday's blocks are filled in. They use the Grind Log's optional Friday cardio as a brisk walk with Dustin, plus the hour you'd normally spend commuting. Dars's optional dance class goes in the early evening.
  10. The morning is split between you two. One walks Dustin while the other showers, then you swap.

"Likely asleep" allows about 20–30 minutes to fall asleep and for brief wake-ups. Every wake time stays within 90 minutes of 5:00, so Monday mornings won't feel like jet lag.

More ideas

  • Pick a light dance style for Friday. Dars's Saturday session is the heaviest glute day of the week. Social styles like salsa or bachata work well the night before. Avoid barre or jump-heavy cardio dance, which tire the same muscles. If a class runs late, it's fine as long as lights out stays near 9:00.
  • Treat the commute home as part of winding down. Listen to a podcast or music, and don't check work messages. The hour then counts as rest instead of more time spent on work.
  • The 7:15 reset is what keeps mornings calm. Gym bag by the door, clothes out, lunch in the fridge. A morning with nothing to decide is how you avoid feeling like you're chasing the next day.
  • Plan for Dustin's long days. On office days he's alone from about 7:30 to 6:00. A midday dog walker once or twice a week, or a longer Wednesday walk, helps.
  • Leave Sunday mostly empty. Only three anchors: a long morning walk, about 2 hours of groceries and prep, and lights out by 9. The rest is yours.
  • If a 5:00 AM is hard, skip the gym, not sleep. Sleep well and do the missed session on Friday instead of the easy cardio.
